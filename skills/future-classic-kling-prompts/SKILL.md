# Kling parked-car camera prompts

Use this shared prompting skill from the finished-car and before/after workflows. Its job is to make a short, reviewable prompt from an inspected shot, not keep appending every past failure to every prompt. Image preparation, source identity, logo/plate geometry, parked-wiper and lighting QA stay in the parent skills. Read [prompting.md](references/prompting.md) for evidence, shot choices, failure handling and tool schema.

## Before writing

Use the parent skill's vehicle-identity-map before new image/Element preparation: original pixel coordinates, source-front N-grid, surfaces, counts, exact readable marks and asymmetry exceptions are reference evidence. Keep the numeric map in still-generation/QA materials; use only relevant source-backed details in the short motion body. Do not transplant source screen coordinates to a new viewpoint. A source-map integrity pass does not pass a generated image.

Inspect the exact Start Frame and all current Element references. Record actual vehicle, restoration stage, source/reference hashes, real Element name and visible critical details. A defective image or Element is held for repair; text instructions cannot turn it into a passed input. For the Jeep, the owner revoked the low-horizontal staging on2026-10-25: preserve the source-documented diagonal rest posture and pivots. Judge every input/Element against those originals; a diagonal angle alone is not a failure. Do not regenerate anything just because this skill is invoked.

Record one `camera` plan: kind, starting-camera reference, physical destination, subject anchor, which face becomes more visible/less visible, framing, extent and pace. LEFT/RIGHT alone is insufficient. Choose the actual physical path from the picture; never automatically move every vehicle left. Orbit means camera translation along an arc plus continuous aiming toward the subject, producing a changed viewpoint and background parallax. It is distinct from sideways tracking, pan-in-place and zoom. Other requested camera moves need an explicit separate plan; do not silently convert them to orbit.

Use the same pace wording, `slow, steady`, for every current shot. Do not alternate `slow`, `very slow` and `gentle` as incidental prose variations. Set total view change separately: tight front-half/macro crops normally need a short arc (`subtle` extent), retaining the anchor and allowing only a slight reveal. Whole-car shots can use `small` extent if their motion corridor is source-supported. This language standard is not a guarantee of identical rendered angular speed; with equal duration a shorter arc implies less average angular displacement. A request toward the front centerline is a direction, not an instruction to reach a complete frontal view. The fast964 front-half is a failed local example, not a reason to slow every shot. Degrees are optional uncalibrated targets; neither2degrees nor6degrees is a universal recipe. Do not add numeric angles without useful evidence or an explicit test rationale.

## Prompt body

Write English. Lead with ONE camera action, followed by its small visible result. Identify the same stationary vehicle via the actual @Element picker token and Start Frame. Keep height/focal length and camera-only movement explicit; record subject distance and expected scene parallax in the plan for output review. Add at most one or two critical visible-detail clauses if needed. Keep the full emblem/lettering/wiper inventory in QA. Only mention parked wipers when visible. An absent emblem need not be introduced into the text at all. New generated video inputs default to the owner-selected plate-free state, consistently across Start Frames and Element references. Do not add Future Classic plate-preservation text to a plate-free shot. If that package explicitly retains plates, existing print remains on its physical mount with natural crop/occlusion; never promise pixel-perfect lettering from wording alone.

Use the owner's four successful964 prompts supplied on2026-10-24 as the current empirical structure: a small arc around a specific existing part/corner and a modest reveal; actual single parked-car Element; same height/focal length; shot-specific existing details; natural perspective/occlusion; lamps off and natural exposure; explicit camera-only movement and stationary car/wheels; one continuous shot without zoom/cut/fade. Keep `slow, steady` wording across new adaptations. The short final exclusion clause is retained from this observed example; positive-only phrasing is not a higher priority than relevant local evidence. Keep location/distance/parallax analysis in the camera plan instead of adding several simultaneous steering instructions to every body. `arc around` can describe the desired orbit; the literal word ORBIT is not mandatory. This is a baseline for testing, not a proven Jeep fix. Do not copy Porsche-specific hardware, marks or wheel design into other vehicles.

Prefer a few clear sentences, roughly60–120words when practical. This is an editorial preference, not a Kling limit or proven optimum. Remove repetition before removing a necessary identity constraint. Prefer desired states such as steady subject size, lamps off and wipers parked; avoid a long list of `no`, `never`, `do not`. If the actual selected interface supports a separate negative prompt, put a concise relevant failure list there; otherwise do not fabricate a field or silently paste the whole list into the positive body. Some failures may still require an explicit short constraint after an observed test; positive phrasing is not a guarantee.

Keep 3seconds,1080p,9:16,one output,Multi-Shot off and audio off in the settings/handoff, rather than repeating all settings as scene content. These are this owner's workflow choices, not universal provider restrictions. Fade/trim/speed edits are local unless explicitly requested otherwise.

Keep unknown cost/tokens unknown. Use the existing generation-spend-ledger if the agent is ever separately authorized to submit. One output for one hypothesis, no automatic retries or unresolved duplicate submission. User submission does not authorize agent retries.

Inspect actual start/intermediate/end/playback for perspective change, parked wheels, speed, identity/plate/wipers and background. Record failure category separately: input defect, missing orbit, excessive orbit, subject motion or identity mutation. Change the smallest relevant cause, not the entire recipe. Fix input/Element first for identity defects. For orbit too fast reduce total reveal/extent before changing all shots or local playback. Request/reuse provider job evidence and debit once. Never claim credit savings or improved Kling performance from a static linter test.

Single-Element clips can still move the car or corrupt plate/body lettering. Never attribute these failures to a two-Element transition without verifying the actual setup. Record owner-reported failures separately from directly inspected playback; an unknown result link/shot stays unknown. For suspected subject motion inspect tire contact and body position against nearby ground across frames, allowing for real camera parallax. Separate rolling/sliding from camera-only viewpoint change. Inspect both Start Frame and Element lettering before diagnosing output mutation. If the source is sound and the output lettering changes, do not certify a longer preservation clause as a fix. Discuss source-supported motion reduction or local tracked restoration of the original artwork only as unverified options; local lettering repair is suitable only if geometry and motion otherwise pass. Hold the failed result and avoid blind paid retries.

For a restrained angle change specify the ending view category as well as direction. A three-quarter clip normally retains both the rear/front face and the same visible side unless a larger reveal is explicitly intended. Do not accidentally make a full frontal/rear destination the objective. In the directly inspected Jeep05 result942935216526606413, a rear-centerline destination ended almost straight behind despite a small-arc phrase. Background perspective also changed, so that clip alone does not prove vehicle rolling or a turntable. A trial can remove the full-view destination and retain the three-quarter view; an explicit vehicle position/orientation relative to the workshop and planted tire contacts may clarify the intended world state. Treat these clauses as one unverified diagnostic candidate, not a new guarantee or a reason to rewrite every shot. Preserve the failed prompt/result and verify one candidate before promoting it to the batch.

The owner identifies the sliding appearance relative to the building/ground as the primary Jeep05 failure, ahead of rotation speed. In the next candidate put the physical scene relationship directly after the camera action: vehicle position and orientation unchanged relative to the building; each tire on the same physical ground patch without rolling/steering/sliding; camera-induced perspective/parallax consistent across vehicle, ground and building. This fixes the intended physical relationship in words, not background pixels on the screen. Never request an orbit while freezing the building/background in image coordinates, or lock tire contacts to screen coordinates. A mild background shift alone is not a pass: inspect whether the car appears to slide/rotate independently of the shared scene. Keep this as an unverified hypothesis until real playback passes, and do not promote it merely because a text check passes.

Before/after: bind only that state's Element; preserve the state already present, with no restoration morph.
