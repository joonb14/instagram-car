# Camera composition reference library

## General optional vocabulary

The E30 selections below are examples, not a fixed six-shot recipe. Offer varied source-supported options: C-pillar/rear quarter glass; A-pillar/windshield corner; roof-to-rear-glass transition; wheel/center cap/fender; headlamp glass; oblique rear half; side decal; mirror/door glass; rear emblem; wide front/rear three-quarter; side-dominant whole car. Mix subject scale, camera height and front/side/rear direction. The tool's `compositions` catalog describes candidates; it is not evidence that reference images exist for each. Do not fabricate reference assets or owner approval. Generate a useful shortlist within authorized scope, show a numbered contact sheet, and let the owner select before video submission. Keep rejected/unselected alternatives out of the final clip sequence. The current 964 side closing is project-specific; future closings can vary.

Read actual reference pixels before applying a template. Assets are in ../assets/composition-templates/; index.json records source and status. They teach camera position, crop, subject size and negative space ONLY. Never put a different car's template in the Kling identity Element or copy its grille, lamp count, wheels, badge, decal, color or exhaust. Images from the new vehicle remain authoritative. A template's selection does not approve its identity fidelity.

Baseline owner-selected E30 compositions: front-half.jpg, front-low.jpg, rear-three-quarter.jpg, whole-car.jpg, side-dominant.jpg. Use only the useful camera language for the new car. Natural half crops can hide badges/plates; do not reposition marks. Whole cars retain margins.
