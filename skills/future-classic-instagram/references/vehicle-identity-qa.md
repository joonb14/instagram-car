## Lighting: owner-selected lights-off / no-reflection style

Weather/location is a per-film creative choice. The E30 wet treatment is not a default for other cars. Review original-photo backgrounds and recent actual exports; bright/dark, green rural roads, factory grounds and harbors are options. Use rain only occasionally and keep each film's setting/weather coherent. See `advertising-reels.md`.

For this treatment extinguish artificial light sources themselves, rather than just painting over their reflections: wall fixtures, streetlights, window/building lights, cabin lights and all vehicle head/auxiliary/fog/parking/indicator/tail/brake lights OFF. Lenses retain their natural material color without emission. Use only soft cool-neutral diffuse sky illumination to retain body and cabin detail. No new spotlight, rim light, bright window or glowing fixture.

Damp ground stays dark and rough with only a weak physically plausible diffuse sky sheen. Reject orange/gold pools, displaced or directionally inconsistent reflections, mirror-like glitter, floating highlights and residual reflections from removed sources. Check body, glazing, wheels and ground together. Do not crush the car to black to meet the darker style. Keep the cabin dry; rain outside does not imply water on the steering wheel or seats. This is the selected campaign treatment, not a universal ban on lamps in every future project; change it only when the owner changes direction.

## Three independent review passes

Review EACH angle independently, especially rear views after any repair. For generated inputs, record `lightingReview: {treatment, reviewedAt, sourceComparison, evidenceFiles, surfaces}`. Each surface entry uses `surface: body|glazing|lamp-lenses|ground|background`, `status`, and a concrete `observation` about visible highlights, source direction, material response, emission and consistency. All five must pass before video submission. A generic "all lights OFF" note is insufficient. Compare the actual whole image plus detail views: rear glass/trunk reflections, lamp glow, bumper highlights and ground glare can regress even while the front remains correct. Changed input hashes invalidate prior review. A requested no-reflection look can fail from excessive diffuse wet shine even without orange light pools. Natural body/glass response is not automatically a defect in other treatments; judge against that shot's recorded style and actual source rather than blindly blacking it out.

When the owner identifies unwanted reflections, immediately withdraw that asset's pass/selection, retain its prior review history, and re-review the saved image before reuse. A source/reference check, script success or previous approval cannot override a visible reported defect. The validator only enforces complete records, file integrity and held/rejected exclusion; it does NOT visually detect reflections. The agent must inspect actual pixels and animation frames. Automated brightness heuristics cannot certify physically correct reflections.

For advertising-film planning, use `advertising-reels.md`: reference mood, camera language and approximate rhythm do not prescribe shot order. Engine bays and other faithful original additions can improve our film even when absent from a reference. Vary future films from real source strengths, while keeping the selected campaign coherent.

1. **Source to still:** compare the whole car and each identity-critical region at full size, not just a thumbnail. Record component geometry, exact text and unresolved details separately from aesthetic approval.
2. **After every edit:** repeat the same checks on the actual final saved file, even after a lighting-only change, upscale, outpaint or plate edit. Recheck both wheel caps, steering crest/letters, front/rear badges and decals. Prior approval does not transfer to changed pixels. Check consistency across shots.
3. **Video and assembled Reel:** inspect playback plus start, middle, end and intermediate frames; review the moving emblems, lettering, lamp mounting and wheels for mutation, flicker and drift. Recheck the encoded assembled MP4, framing and transitions. Stationary-car orbit shots must not spin wheels or rotate the car itself. Whole-car shots must retain both bumpers and visible wheels throughout.

For stationary lights-off cabin shots, also inspect warning lamps, instrument needles, selector position and steering angle over time.
