This explicit owner requirement is not covered by routine autonomous design approvals. Do not generate a multi-angle batch while confirmation is pending or carry another vehicle's background approval forward. If the background is rejected, hold all affected images and exclude them from Elements/videos; retain originals and drafts. The Jeep gray concrete courtyard trial was rejected on2026-10-23.

Choose from varied source-supported camera options, not a fixed three- or six-shot list. Front, front three-quarter and rear three-quarter are starter examples. C-pillar, roofline, wheel, lamp, mirror, rear half, badge or other details are optional when actual before AND after evidence supports comparison. Show candidate pairs for owner selection; make the final order explicit. Never invent damage or an undocumented pre-restoration configuration merely to complete a pair.

Create TWO distinct Kling Elements: one containing only pre-restoration exterior references, one containing only completed-restoration exterior references. Each uses four complementary, individually reviewed exterior images and its own concise description/name. Never mix state references or bind both Elements to a clip. The owner rejected the within-clip two-Element transformation experiment on2026-10-24; use separate clips and local cuts.
